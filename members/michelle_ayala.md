## Introduction

I'm Michelle Ayala, a student majoring in entrepreneurship and marketing. In this project, I worked on the Everyman, Lover, and Jester archetypes, the Art Deco, Punk, and Grunge design styles, and the Liking and Commitment & Consistency persuasion principles.

## My archetypes

I was assigned three brand archetypes for this project:

* **Everyman:** Down-to-earth and relatable. Brands using this archetype want people to feel they belong and are treated like regular people.
* **Lover:** Focused on warmth, intimacy, and connection. These brands make people feel appreciated and special.
* **Jester:** Playful, funny, and lighthearted. These brands add joy and humor to everyday life.

Each archetype has a postmodernist and a modernist hero design in `assets/heroes/`, paired with the Liking and Commitment & Consistency persuasion principles.
